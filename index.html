    Flight rules, assumptions & sources

    Each turn represents 10 minutes in an accelerated mission. A coastal measurement samples a new modeled visit to the same site. Each acquired record is tagged with its visit or compartment. Downlink sends complete records in acquisition order. The radiation scenario compares coverage across compartments, without predicting radiation damage. Custom experiments use a generic record model.

    Each step balances energy in Wh using (supply − load) × 1/6 hour. The simplified daylight supply uses the M12P Mid's published 29.5 W Sun-tracking payload power as a teaching reference. Eclipse supply is zero. Active payload power, standby (6 W), downlink (15 W), battery capacities, sunlight schedule, data volumes and contact limits are illustrative game assumptions. The battery supplies the difference when demand exceeds supply. Battery depletion ends the mission.
